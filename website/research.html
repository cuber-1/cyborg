The question driving our work

When the robot helps,
what happens to the human?

We’re studying the moment physical assistance changes attention, effort, and engagement.

The tension

Easier movement does not always mean less mental work.

An exoskeleton can take load off the body. But the person may still be planning, correcting, and paying close attention—or they may begin to disengage.

We want to tell those situations apart.
