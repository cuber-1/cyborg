Research

What changes when
a robot lends a hand?

We’re investigating how exoskeleton assistance affects mental workload, movement, and engagement.

Study design in development

The question

Robotic assistance can make a movement easier. We want to understand how it changes the person’s attention, effort, and active contribution.

Our goal is to identify useful measures of mental workload during assisted movement, then investigate how they could inform assistance.

What we plan to measure

Brain activity

Compare EEG features, including alpha and theta power, to see which reflect cognitive demand during movement.

EEG & workload papers ↗

Movement

Measure accuracy, timing, and physical effort to understand performance and the person’s contribution.

Robotic assistance papers ↗

Experience

Ask participants about perceived workload, engagement, and confidence alongside their task performance.
